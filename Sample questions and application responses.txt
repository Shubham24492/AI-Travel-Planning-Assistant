Question 1: Which neighbourhoods are suitable for cultural experiences?

Application response: 

Answer
Knowledge-base facts
Chinatown: Known for its heritage and food.
Kampong Glam: Suited for visitors interested in Malay culture, art, and cafes.
Little India: Offers colorful streets, local food, and shopping.
Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay: Suitable for a scenic evening itinerary. [Source 1]

No current data available.
Recommendation
For cultural experiences in Singapore, I recommend exploring:

Chinatown for its rich heritage and diverse food options.
Kampong Glam to immerse yourself in Malay culture and enjoy art and cafes.
Little India for vibrant streets and authentic local cuisine.
These neighborhoods provide a great mix of cultural experiences and are easily accessible via public transport.
