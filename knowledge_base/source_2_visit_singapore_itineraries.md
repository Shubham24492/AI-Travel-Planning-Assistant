## Suggested 3-day outline
A relaxed 3-day trip can mix heritage discovery, urban attractions, and a family-friendly experience. Day 1 can focus on Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, and a sunset view. Day 2 can explore Chinatown, Kampong Glam, and Little India with food and cultural stops. Day 3 can include museums, indoor attractions, or a beach and island experience such as Sentosa.

## Family-friendly ideas
Families with children can combine Singapore Zoo, the Singapore River Cruise, Gardens by the Bay, and indoor attractions such as the National Aquarium, interactive museums, or shopping centres with activity zones.

## Cultural neighbourhoods
Chinatown is known for heritage and food; Kampong Glam suits visitors interested in Malay culture, art, and cafes; Little India offers colourful streets, local food, and shopping. Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay suit a scenic evening itinerary.

## Practical guidance
Travelers should allow time for rest between outdoor sightseeing blocks, especially in the afternoon heat. Intermix outdoor experiences with indoor activities for comfort and to manage weather changes.
