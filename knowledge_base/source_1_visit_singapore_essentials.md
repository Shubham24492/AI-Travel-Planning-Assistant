## Singapore at a glance
Singapore is a compact city-state with varied neighbourhoods, efficient public transport, and a mix of heritage districts, contemporary parks, and cultural enclaves. Visitors can enjoy both outdoor spaces and indoor attractions across the island.

## Transport and neighbourhoods
The city has a highly connected MRT network, buses, and taxis. Popular areas for culture and food include Chinatown, Kampong Glam, Little India, and the Marina Bay area. These neighbourhoods offer heritage, shopping, and dining experiences in a walkable layout.

## Weather and climate
Singapore is warm and humid year-round, with tropical weather. The island experiences occasional heavy rain and a wet season, which makes indoor attractions useful on rainy afternoons. Travellers are advised to check the current forecast before planning outdoor walking-heavy itineraries.

## Travel tips
Carry water, use MRT for faster city movement, and plan indoor activities for stormy afternoons. Public spaces, museums, and shopping centres are comfortable options during heat or rain.

## Attractions and experiences
Popular attractions include Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay Sands, Singapore Zoo, National Gallery Singapore, and Sentosa. Outdoor lovers can explore East Coast Park, the Singapore River, and the city’s park connectors, while indoor options include museums, shopping centres, and aquarium experiences.
