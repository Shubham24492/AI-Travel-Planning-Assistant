## Overview
Singapore is a modern, multicultural city-state with a diverse population and a strong focus on public transit, cleanliness, and high-density urban planning. It blends historic neighborhoods, green spaces, and contemporary waterfront attractions.

## Major attractions
Must-visit attractions include Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, Merlion Park, Chinatown, Kampong Glam, Little India, the Botanic Gardens, and Sentosa. The city also has strong choices for museums, local food, and heritage walks.

## Food and local experience
Singapore is famous for hawker centres, local coffee shops, and ethnic cuisine spanning Chinese, Malay, Indian, and other influences. Visitors are encouraged to try local favourites and neighbourhood food centres rather than only tourist restaurants.

## Getting around
The public transportation system is fast and reliable, with MRT and buses connecting most major tourist areas. For a first-time visitor, the MRT is the easiest way to move between districts and to access major attractions.

## Cultural norms and practical guidance
Tourists should respect local customs, dress modestly in religious places, and stay hydrated. Alternative indoor plans are helpful during hot afternoons or rainy weather. The city is generally safe and easy to navigate for first-time visitors.
