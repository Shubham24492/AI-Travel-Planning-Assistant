# Source 4: Visit Singapore - Top Things To Do
Source title: Visit Singapore - Top Things To Do
URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/things-to-do/top-things-to-do/

## Experience categories
Visit Singapore groups activities into unique experiences, city in nature, culture and heritage, iconic architecture, family fun, after dark, museums and galleries, shopping, and dining. The destination site also highlights featured neighbourhoods including Chinatown, the Civic District, Kampong Gelam, Katong-Joo Chiat, Little India, Mandai, Marina Bay, Orchard Road, Sentosa Island, and the Singapore River.

## Planning use
The official destination site is useful for matching traveller interests to activity types. For a family, look at family-focused attractions and indoor experiences. For culture, combine heritage neighbourhoods with museums and galleries. For nature, consider parks, Mandai, Pulau Ubin, or other city-in-nature experiences, then confirm opening times directly with the attraction.
